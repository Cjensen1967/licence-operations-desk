APPLICATION INFORMATION · LOCAL WORKSPACE

Licence Operations Desk

Version 1.0.5 workspace · Published manifest baseline 1.0.4

Operational purpose & local privacy

Licence Operations Desk organizes public Washington State gambling-licence records returned by the Washington State Gambling Commission (WSGC) public License Lookup into an attention-first local workspace.

Managed properties, accepted roster snapshots, change comparisons, My Staff flags, departments, and local tags are stored locally in this browser profile. Network requests are user-initiated only.

Independence & regulatory scope

Licence Operations Desk is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WSGC. Public lookup observations and local organizational labels are review aids, not employment, licensing, or enforcement determinations.
