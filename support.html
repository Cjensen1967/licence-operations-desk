Licence Operations Desk Support

Support and information for the Licence Operations Desk browser extension.

About

Licence Operations Desk is a local browser workspace for organizing and reviewing public Washington State gambling-license information returned through the Washington State Gambling Commission (WSGC) public License Lookup.

Getting help

If you experience a problem, please email cjensen1967@gmail.com. Include a brief description of what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Please do not send passwords, account credentials, or other sensitive information.

Privacy

Read the Licence Operations Desk Privacy Policy for information about local storage, WSGC requests, and data handling.

WSGC availability

Licence Operations Desk depends on WSGC's public License Lookup. Changes, maintenance, or outages affecting the WSGC website may temporarily affect lookup or roster-refresh functions.

Independent project

Licence Operations Desk is an independent project. It is not an official Washington State Gambling Commission product and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by WSGC.
