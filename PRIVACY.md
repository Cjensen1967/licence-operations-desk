# Privacy Policy — Licence Operations Desk

**Effective date: September 21, 2026**

Licence Operations Desk helps users search, organize, compare, and report public Washington State gambling-license information in a local browser workspace.

## Information processed

When the user deliberately performs a lookup or roster refresh, the extension sends the entered public search criteria or selected WSGC organization identifier to the Washington State Gambling Commission website. The browser may send session cookies and ordinary request metadata to WSGC under WSGC’s own privacy practices.

Returned public records may include names, government license identifiers, organization information, business locations, license types, statuses, effective dates, expiration dates, and related public source fields.

## Local storage

Managed properties, accepted roster snapshots, retrieval outcomes, preferences, and user-restored backups are stored locally in the browser profile. Temporary search results and request-verification tokens are not intentionally persisted by the application. Exported reports and backups are saved only to locations selected by the user.

## No developer collection

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, tracking, account system, or cloud synchronization. The developer does not receive the user’s searches, rosters, saved workspace, reports, or backups through the extension.

## Sharing, retention and deletion

The extension does not sell user data or share it for advertising or unrelated purposes. Information is transmitted to WSGC only for the public lookup requested by the user. Local records remain until removed through the application, browser-profile controls, or extension removal. The workspace provides export, restore, property-removal, and complete local-deletion controls.

## Security and limitations

The extension restricts network access to the WSGC account website and uses packaged application code. Users control exported files and should store them appropriately. This independent tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by WSGC. Public results may be delayed, incomplete, or changed by the source. The application does not determine employment, licensing eligibility, enforcement status, or legal compliance.

## Contact

A support contact and public policy URL must be entered in the Chrome Web Store listing before publication.
